# Lunascape Mobile User Guide

Lunascape Mobile is a powerful, feature-rich mobile browser that combines traditional web browsing with cutting-edge Web3 capabilities. Built for modern users who demand both performance and innovation, it brings blockchain technology, news aggregation and advanced browsing features together in a single, intuitive interface.

## Download Lunascape Mobile

An ad-blocking browser app made in Japan, with a built-in Web3 wallet that connects seamlessly to DApps such as Uniswap, so you can buy and sell cryptocurrencies and NFTs with ease.

- **iOS** — [Get it on the App Store](http://itunes.apple.com/app/ilunascape/id380065805?mt=8)
- **Android** — [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.lunascape.android.ilunascape)

See [Download and install](getting-started/download.md) for the steps, and [Troubleshooting](getting-started/troubleshooting.md) if something does not work.

## Lunascape Mobile v15 features

- **Blocks video ads too** — complete blocking of website and video advertisements
- **Integrated Web3 wallet** — an EVM wallet supporting Ethereum and Japan Open Chain
- **Integrated RSS news reader** — browse your favorite information in one place, inside the browser
- **20+ years of experience** — a Japanese browser with over 20 million downloads
- **Privacy-focused** — a secure design with no tracking and no collection of personal data

## Overview

Lunascape Mobile focuses on delivering an exceptional experience across every part of mobile browsing — from basic web navigation to advanced Web3 interactions, news reading and privacy features. It is designed to be easy to use while offering powerful functionality to casual users and blockchain enthusiasts alike.

### One app, multiple capabilities

Lunascape Mobile is the only mobile browser that seamlessly integrates traditional web browsing with a Web3 wallet, news aggregation and advanced privacy features. Whether you are browsing the web, managing cryptocurrency assets or keeping up with the news, everything works together in one unified experience.

### A focus on performance and security

Lunascape is built to perform excellently on modern mobile devices, with efficient hardware acceleration, optimized Web3 interactions and robust privacy protection. Your browsing data and cryptocurrency assets are secured with industry-standard encryption and self-custody wallet technology.

### Clean, intuitive, feature-rich design

Lunascape is designed to work beautifully across mobile devices and platforms. Carefully crafted UI components, a responsive layout and adaptive theming across screen sizes give you a premium browsing experience.

### Web and Web3 optimized

Lunascape combines the full power of modern web standards with cutting-edge Web3 capabilities, bringing together traditional browsing with decentralized applications, cryptocurrency management and blockchain interactions.
