# Lunascape Wallet User Guide

Lunascape Wallet is a Web3 wallet extension. On EVM-compatible chains such as Ethereum and Japan Open Chain, you can hold and send crypto assets (tokens) and NFTs, and connect to Web3 sites (DApps).

It comes built into Lunascape Desktop. In Google Chrome and other browsers that run Chrome extensions, add it from the Chrome Web Store.

## Main features

- Create a new wallet, or bring in an existing one with its 12-word seed phrase.
- Send and receive tokens (with QR codes), and check balances and prices.
- View and transfer NFTs.
- Works with Ethereum and Japan Open Chain out of the box; add other EVM-compatible networks yourself.
- Keep several accounts in one wallet, including accounts imported with a private key and Ledger accounts.
- Review a site's requests to connect, sign or add a network on a confirmation screen before you allow them.

## Before you start

Lunascape Wallet keeps the keys that move your assets only on your computer. Nobody — including the company behind Lunascape — holds them for you.

- If you lose your seed phrase, the wallet cannot be restored. [Back it up](../../getting-started/i18n/en/backup.md) as soon as you create the wallet.
- Never tell anyone your seed phrase, private key or password. Lunascape support will never ask for them.
- A transaction sent to the blockchain cannot be reversed. Check the recipient, network and amount before you send.
