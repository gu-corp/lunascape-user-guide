# Troubleshooting

## I forgot my master password

The password can't be recovered. If you have your seed phrase, set the wallet up again with it. You can also change the password from **Change Master Password** in the settings, which asks for the seed phrase. Without the seed phrase, the wallet can't be unlocked.

## I can't send / "Insufficient balance" appears

You need the network's own currency for the fee (ETH on Ethereum, JOC on Japan Open Chain), separately from the token you send. Receive a little of it, or [buy it](assets.md#buy-a-token), and try again.

## A token I received doesn't show up

Check that the sender used a network in your wallet. If it isn't in the asset list, [add the token](assets.md#add-tokens).

## Face ID doesn't work

After a fresh install, the Face ID switch may not appear when you register your password. The app may ask later, on the lock screen, whether to register Face ID. Until then, unlock with the master password.

## A site doesn't find the wallet

- Make sure you've opened the site in Lunascape Browser, not in another browser.
- Choose MetaMask on the site, or turn on **Enable ethereum.isMetaMask** in the settings and reload.

## I see the name "G.U. Wallet"

Some confirmation screens still show the wallet's former name. It's the same wallet, Lunascape Wallet.

## Before you contact us

Never send your seed phrase, private key or password to anyone, including Lunascape support. Include the app version, your device, the network and what you were doing.

- [Support](https://www.lunascape.org/en/support)
