## Bundled software

The extension bundles the following open-source software. The full license texts and generated notices are included under the extension's `vendor/` folder and in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.

- Marked
- DOMPurify 3.4.14
- Mermaid
- KaTeX
- Turndown and Turndown Plugin GFM
- docs-lint (Editor runtime)
- @lunascape/tikz-renderer (MIT)
- Vega and Vega-Lite
- Markmap
- WaveDrom
- Svgbob
- Penrose Core (experimental)

node-tikzjax and its DVI converter, TeX assets and fonts required for TikZ rendering are development-only and are not included in the distributed build.
