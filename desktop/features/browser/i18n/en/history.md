# History

## See where you have been

Choose **History** from the menu (≡), or press `Ctrl+H` / `Cmd+H`. Pages are listed by date. The search box filters by title and URL.

## Delete history

Remove entries one at a time from the list. To clear it in bulk, use the browsing-data controls in **Settings** > **Privacy**.

## How long history is kept

**Settings** > **Privacy** > **History retention** sets how many days history is kept. Anything older is deleted automatically.

> **Note:** Pages opened in a private window are not recorded in history.
